India News Today — 31 August 2026

This file contains a short roundup of selected India news reported on 31 August 2026.

1. India's GDP growth
India's real GDP grew by 7.8% in the first quarter of financial year 2026-27, compared with 6.9% in the same period of the previous financial year. Real GVA growth was assessed at 8.2%.

2. India's fiscal deficit
India's fiscal deficit for April-July stood at about ₹4.55 trillion, equal to 26.8% of the full-year target for financial year 2026-27. Government capital expenditure during the period was about ₹4.5 trillion.

3. Hottest August since 1901
The India Meteorological Department said August 2026 was India's hottest August since 1901, with a mean temperature of 28.01°C. September rainfall is expected to be below normal.

4. Manipur Census 2027
The Centre deferred the Census exercise in Manipur amid demands related to the National Register of Citizens (NRC).

5. Gujarat gutkha and paan masala ban
The Gujarat government extended its ban on the manufacture, sale, storage and distribution of gutkha and tobacco-based paan masala until September 2027.
